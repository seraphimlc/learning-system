# Mastery Evaluation Knowledge Pack v1

- Evaluate six dimensions: concept, model_relation, procedure, calculation, expression_notation, transfer.
- Usable live evidence may support a status update; mock, stale, pending, rejected, or missing-lineage evidence cannot.
- One correct direct answer can be emerging evidence, not durable mastery.
- Weak reasoning with a correct answer remains weak until varied usable evidence confirms transfer.
- Planner signals should describe the next evidence need, not choose concrete questions.
