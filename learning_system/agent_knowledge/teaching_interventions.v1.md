# Teaching Interventions Knowledge Pack v1

- Keep child copy concrete, calm, and non-shaming.
- Wrong-answer repair names the first wrong relation or step, then gives one corrected move.
- Worked examples should be tiny and structurally aligned, with no hidden leaps.
- Clarification asks for the missing evidence and permits text, photo, or cannot-provide.
- Do not claim mastery after teaching; the next evidence step must confirm learning.
